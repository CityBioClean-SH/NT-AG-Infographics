How to Read These Numbers
Comparing a single church to an entire district or national body requires normalizing the data. Raw totals aren't meaningful — of course 42,749 people attend more churches than 1,105 attend one.

Charts below use rates per 100 worship attendees so every entity is on equal footing. For example, if a church has 10 conversions per 100 attendees and the district has 8, the church is converting at a higher rate regardless of size.

Health ratios (AC, CW, AW, CS, AS) are already rate-based by design — they measure how many people it takes to produce one outcome (e.g., one conversion, one baptism). Lower ratios = healthier. These are the standard AG church health metrics used across every district.

Strengths & Areas for Growth

Where Russellville First AG excels and where there's room to improve

▲ Strengths

  • CS ratio (4.79) is close to the healthy range of 3–4 and significantly better than both the Gulf Area (6.67) and National (6.58) averages. Spirit baptisms are happening relative to conversions.
  • Attendance of 1,105 is nearly 10x the Arkansas median of 54 and about 9.5x the district mean of 116 — one of the state's largest AG churches.
  • 187 conversions means people are responding to the gospel. The raw conversion number reflects an active, evangelistic culture.

▼ Areas for Growth

  • CW ratio (4.92) — converts are not getting water baptized at the rate the district does (1.71). Nearly 3x the national average of 3.15. A stronger pathway from decision to baptism would close this gap.
  • AW ratio (29.08) — nearly 3x the district average (10.97) and national average (11.15). For every 29 people in the pew, only 1 is getting water baptized.
  • AS ratio (28.33) — above the healthy goal of 20 and higher than both Arkansas (26.60) and National (23.32). More emphasis on Spirit-empowered life for the whole congregation.
  • AC ratio (5.91) — slightly above the healthy threshold of 5.0, though better than the Arkansas district (6.42). More intentional outreach moments could push this into the green.
